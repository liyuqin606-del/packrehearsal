# Code of conduct

PackRehearsal is committed to a respectful, technically rigorous community.

Be constructive, assume good faith, distinguish evidence from opinion, and make
space for people with different levels of packaging experience. Harassment,
threats, discrimination, deliberate disclosure of private information, and
repeated bad-faith disruption are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these
expectations and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation. For a
confidential conduct report, use the repository's private vulnerability
reporting form and begin the report title with `[conduct]`. Reports will be
handled discreetly and without retaliation.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Critique behavior and artifacts, not people.
